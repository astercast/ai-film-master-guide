# Direct gaze and eyelines

## Use when

Dialogue and ensemble scenes.

## Inputs

- Blocking plan
- Who looks at whom, when

## Procedure

1. Write the eyeline map: character → target, per beat.
2. Place it BEFORE the blocking block in the prompt.
3. Dead eyes are fixed by giving eyes a job, never by lighting.

## Worked example

GAZE: Mara holds on Vex's hands until 'the ledger' — then snaps to his eyes.

## Pitfalls

- 'She looks sad' — an emotion label, not an eyeline. Give the eyes a target.
