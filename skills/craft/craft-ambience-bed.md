# Lay the ambience bed

## Use when

Every production, from minute one of post.

## Inputs

- Location recordings or generated ambience per location

## Procedure

1. One bed per location, continuous under all its scenes.
2. Room tone under everything — silence in AI film reads as a bug.
3. Crossfade beds at location changes; never hard-cut ambience.

## Worked example

Kitchen bed runs 6 minutes straight under 14 shots — the scene feels like one room.

## Pitfalls

- Per-shot ambience — the room changes every cut.
