# Draft at 720p, upscale winners

## Use when

All generation until selects lock.

## Inputs

- Shotlist

## Procedure

1. Everything drafts at 720p (or 480p for blocking questions).
2. Selects get hero renders; non-selects never see 4K.
3. Upscale is a finishing step, not a generation step.

## Worked example

60-shot short: 60 drafts at 720p → 22 heroes at 1080p+. Budget halved.

## API references

- `data/v1/models.json`

## Pitfalls

- 4K drafts 'to judge properly' — judge at 720p, spend at 4K.
