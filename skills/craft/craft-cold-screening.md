# Screen for cold viewers

## What this gets you

You will find the confusing, boring and broken moments you have gone blind to — by testing the cut on fresh viewers before it ships.

## Use when

Fine cut stage.

## Inputs

- Fine cut
- 3–5 viewers who know nothing about the film

## Procedure

1. Screen without introduction or defense.
2. Watch where they check out — that's the cut note.
3. Ask what they felt, not what they thought.
4. Cut what they don't feel, even if you love the shot.

## Worked example

Cold screening killed a beloved 20s drone shot — the film got 20s better.

## Pitfalls

- Screening for friends who'll be nice — recruit strangers.
