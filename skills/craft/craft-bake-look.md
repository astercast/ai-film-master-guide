# Bake the look into plates

## Use when

Any recurring location or look.

## Inputs

- Location plates

## Procedure

1. Generate the plate WITH the grade, lens character, and texture baked in.
2. Video prompts carry only what changes (action, blocking).
3. Anything the video model keeps dropping belongs in the plate.
4. The plate itself becomes the lens.

## Worked example

Anamorphic kitchen: squeeze + oval bokeh baked into the plate — 14 shots, one look.

## Pitfalls

- Repeating lens language in every video prompt — it drifts; the plate doesn't.
