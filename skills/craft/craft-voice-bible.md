# Maintain the Voice Bible

## Use when

Recurring speaking characters.

## Inputs

- Voice specs per character

## Procedure

1. Write register/timbre/tempo/manner once.
2. Paste verbatim into every generation — no synonyms, no paraphrase.
3. Separate dialogue and audio blocks; audio carries voice identity only.

## Worked example

40 generations, one pasted block, one voice.

## Pitfalls

- 'Improving' the wording mid-production — that's a new voice.
