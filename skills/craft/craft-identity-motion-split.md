# Separate identity from motion

## What this gets you

Your characters will stop changing appearance between shots, because who they are and what they do are written separately in every prompt.

## Use when

Every prompt with a recurring subject.

## Inputs

- Identity descriptors
- The shot's action

## Procedure

1. Identity Block: static facts only (face, wardrobe, palette).
2. Motion Block: one action, one camera move.
3. Never pronouns alone as the subject anchor.
4. At long-form scale, this separation is the whole continuity system.

## Worked example

IDENTITY: MARA… / MOTION: crosses the kitchen, sets down the ledger. Slow dolly-in.

## Pitfalls

- Appearance in the motion block — the model will redesign the character mid-action.
