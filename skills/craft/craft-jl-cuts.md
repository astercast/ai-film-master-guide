# Cut with J/L cuts

## What this gets you

Your cuts will flow: dialogue starting before the picture changes (and lingering after) hides edits and makes AI footage feel continuous.

## Use when

Every dialogue scene and most transitions.

## Inputs

- Locked dialogue edit

## Procedure

1. J-cut: hear the next scene before you see it.
2. L-cut: let dialogue trail over the next shot.
3. In AI film, J/L cuts + shared ambience mask more sins than any re-roll.
4. Never let a cut go dry — room tone under everything.

## Worked example

Kitchen → street: street ambience enters 2s before the cut; dialogue trails 1s after.

## Pitfalls

- Hard-cutting every transition — the edit will feel like a slideshow.
