# Re-anchor references

## What this gets you

Before a character's face drifts too far, you will have refreshed their reference, drift fixed while it is still invisible to audiences.

## Use when

Any sequence longer than a few generations.

## Inputs

- Original registry anchors

## Procedure

1. Every 3 to 5 chained generations, re-attach the ORIGINAL references.
2. Don't wait for visible drift, by then it's in three shots.
3. Compare current frame vs anchor side by side; fix at the first 5% shift.

## Worked example

Scene 6: re-anchored at generations 4 and 8, drift caught at 5%, invisible in the cut.

## Pitfalls

- Trusting chained references alone, chains drift, anchors don't.
