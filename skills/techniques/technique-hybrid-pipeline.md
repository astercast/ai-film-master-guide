# Build a hybrid pipeline

## What this gets you

You will have a plan where the emotional moments are filmed for real and AI builds the impossible parts around them, feelings that land, spectacle that still happens.

## Use when

When the film needs emotional weight AI alone can't carry: a real performance at the centre, AI for everything the budget couldn't shoot.

## Inputs

- Storyboards (paper or digital)
- Previz (Blender or phone footage)
- Real performance or artwork for the emotional core

## Procedure

1. Board first, if it doesn't work as boards, it won't work as generations.
2. Shoot/capture anything with a human body for real, even as phone previz.
3. Use video-to-video / motion transfer for transformation, not text-to-video for invention.
4. Keep one truth anchor per scene; let AI handle the other 80%.

## Worked example

Get Me Out: real performer + Luma environment capture, re-skinned via video-to-video, Grand Prix.

## Pitfalls

- Generating the emotional core from scratch, the uncanny valley lives there.
