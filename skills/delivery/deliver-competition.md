# Deliver for competitions

## What this gets you

You will have a finished film file that meets competition specs exactly, right format, watermark, packshot, ready to upload.

## Use when

Final delivery to a competition.

## Inputs

- Locked cut
- Competition spec (ratio, codec, watermark rules)

## Procedure

1. Master 16:9 or 21:9 per the competition (never crop in the competition's player, compose for it).
2. MP4/MOV up to 4K; broadcast-safe levels; -1 dBTP ceiling.
3. Burn in the required watermark; design the packshot as a judging touchpoint.
4. QC on a big screen: morphs invisible on a laptop appear at 4K.

## Worked example

21:9, ProRes master → H.264 delivery, watermark lower-third, packshot with title art + credits.

## API references

- `data/v1/competitions.json`
- `data/v1/checklists.json`

## Pitfalls

- Delivering 16:9 composed for 9:16, compose for the delivery ratio from shot one.
