# Deliver for social (9:16)

## Use when

Reels/Shorts/TikTok versions.

## Inputs

- 16:9 master
- Vertical safe-area plan

## Procedure

1. Decide vertical framing per shot at the shotlist stage — post-cropping destroys composition.
2. Protect faces and action in the center 50% horizontally.
3. Reframe (don't just crop): punch-ins per shot beat one global crop.
4. Loud first 3 seconds — the hook is the thumbnail.

## Worked example

16:9 short → 9:16: per-shot reframes, faces protected, hook recut to 0:00–0:03.

## Pitfalls

- One global center-crop — heads get cut, action leaves frame.
