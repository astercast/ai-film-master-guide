# Decide AI vs hybrid performance

## Use when

Planning any scene whose job is one complicated feeling.

## Inputs

- The scene's dramatic job

## Procedure

1. Ask: is this scene's whole job one human feeling something complicated?
2. If yes: shoot/voice it for real, transform with AI (see Get Me Out, Dreams of Violets).
3. If no: direct AI with tactics, eyelines, and rationed close-ups.
4. Never let budget decide this — a failed emotional core sinks the film.

## Worked example

Grief monologue: director voiced it himself, AI-converted timbre per character — human performance, synthetic voice.

## Pitfalls

- AI-generating the one scene the whole film depends on — that's the scene to shoot real.
