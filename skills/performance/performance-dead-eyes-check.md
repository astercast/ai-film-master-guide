# Run the dead-eyes checklist

## Use when

Reviewing any close-up or dialogue coverage.

## Inputs

- The rendered take

## Procedure

1. Microsaccades: do the eyes make 1–2 involuntary movements per second, or hold a mathematical predator stare?
2. Duchenne check: does joy crinkle the eyes, or move only the mouth (the deceptive Pan Am smile)?
3. Asymmetry: is the face hyper-symmetrical (averaged, artificial) or does it have natural irregularity?
4. Pupils: do they respond to light, or sit static (soul blindness)?
5. Fix: prompt imperfection (asymmetry, blemishes, irregular blinking) or correct in post — perfect symmetry is the tell.

## Worked example

Hero CU failed all four — re-prompted with 'slight natural asymmetry, irregular blink' and it passed.

## Pitfalls

- Fixing dead eyes with lighting — they're fixed by giving the eyes a job, not by relighting.
