# Ration close-ups

## What this gets you

Your close-ups will land like punches, because they are saved for the emotional peaks instead of spent everywhere.

## Use when

Planning coverage for any scene.

## Inputs

- The scene's emotional map

## Procedure

1. Mark the 1–3 emotional peaks — those get the close-ups.
2. Everything else: mediums and wides, which read as confident filmmaking.
3. Micro-acting is the weakest link — don't ask for what models can't give.
4. The listener's reaction shot is exempt — AI renders listening well.

## Worked example

8-line scene: 1 CU at the confession line, listener CUs for reactions, rest in OTS/medium.

## Pitfalls

- Close-upping the whole scene — maximum exposure of the weakest capability.
