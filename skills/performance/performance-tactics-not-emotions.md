# Rewrite emotions as tactics

## What this gets you

Your characters will act believably, because you will direct them with things they can DO ('she hides the letter') instead of feelings models cannot render ('she is sad').

## Use when

Every performance beat in every prompt.

## Inputs

- The emotional beat

## Procedure

1. Name the tactic: what is the character TRYING to do? (to warn, to hide, to win).
2. Give it a physical channel: jaw, hands, eyes, weight, breath.
3. Write the tactic + channel, delete the emotion word.
4. Test at 720p: does the tactic read without the label?

## Worked example

Bad: 'she looks devastated.' Good: 'She is trying not to cry in front of him, jaw set, eyes fixed on the door.'

## Pitfalls

- Adjectives are not direction, if you can't act it, the model can't render it.
