# Release on YouTube safely

## What this gets you

Your YouTube release will be set up safely: AI disclosure done right, monetisation protected, no avoidable strikes.

## Use when

Uploading any AI film to YouTube.

## Inputs

- The finished film
- The channel

## Procedure

1. Set the 'Altered content' AI-use toggle in YouTube Studio for realistic AI-generated or altered content, the platform will label it itself if you don't.
2. Disclosure is transparency, not penalty: it doesn't by itself limit reach or monetization.
3. Check the mass-produced-content risk: one authored film on a real channel is fine; fifty near-identical AI shorts on a farm channel is not.
4. Never use AI personas as fake experts on health/legal/finance/politics, unmonetizable.
5. Review the channel as a whole (theme, top videos, thumbnails, About) the way a reviewer would.

## Worked example

Short film upload: toggle set, description notes AI process, monetized, no issues.

## Pitfalls

- Skipping the toggle on realistic content, removal or YPP suspension.
