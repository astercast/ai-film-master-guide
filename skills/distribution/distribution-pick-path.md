# Choose the release path

## What this gets you

You will choose where your film actually gets seen — AI-native distributor, YouTube, festivals, or your own site — with the trade-offs in plain view.

## Use when

When the film is finished and needs an audience.

## Inputs

- The finished film
- Your audience goal

## Procedure

1. Series/CTV-friendly content: evaluate AI-native distributors (Escape.AI et al.) — check curation terms, revenue split, IP retention.
2. Feature or audience-building: YouTube self-release (Cully Hill Boys template) — set disclosure toggles, check channel health against the mass-produced-content rule.
3. Festival-placed shorts/features with press: traditional indie — full deliverables stack + E&O before any distributor conversation.
4. Niche/direct: self-hosted (Vimeo OTT, own site) — you are the distributor; bring your own audience.
5. Do not plan around a major-streamer acquisition of an AI feature — no verified 2026 precedent exists.

## Worked example

110-min AI feature: YouTube free release + open-sourced prompts — audience and credibility in one move.

## Pitfalls

- Chasing a streamer deal that doesn't exist yet — build the audience you can reach.
