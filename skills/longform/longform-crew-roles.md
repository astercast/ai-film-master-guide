# Staff a small AI feature crew

## What this gets you

If your production grows, you will know which jobs exist, what each one owns, and who to give them to — so nothing important is nobody's job.

## Use when

Planning team for a 10+ minute production.

## Inputs

- Runtime target
- Budget
- In-house skills

## Procedure

1. Cover the functions, not the headcount: writer, director(s), asset/continuity manager, prompt writer, editor, color, sound/music.
2. Smallest observed: solo creator (12-min short). Pilot scale: 4 people. Feature scale: 15–28.
3. 40% of one feature crew had never used AI — hire directorial judgment, train the tools.
4. Give continuity its own owner — it's a full-time job past 10 minutes.

## Worked example

20-min short crew of 5: writer-director, asset/continuity manager, prompt writer, editor, sound designer.

## Pitfalls

- No continuity owner — the single most common long-form staffing mistake.
