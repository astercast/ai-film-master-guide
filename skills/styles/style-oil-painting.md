# Direct living oil painting

## Use when

Arthouse entries, music-driven pieces.

## Inputs

- Painting references
- The emotional core

## Procedure

1. Thick impasto language in the style lock; brushwork direction per shot.
2. Motion as paint: strokes smear and reform — the medium IS the motion.
3. Unify with a painted grade pass; keep edges alive, never vector-clean.
4. Let the style forgive: painterly motion hides seams photorealism can't.

## Worked example

Fish's final day: every frame a painting, strokes flowing with the current.
