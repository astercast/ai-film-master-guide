# Direct claymation

## What this gets you

Your film will feel handmade, fingerprints, stepped motion, real-clay charm, instead of computer-smooth.

## Use when

Tactile comedy and charm pieces.

## Inputs

- Character maquettes (or their AI equivalents)
- Miniature set design

## Procedure

1. Stepped motion: holds and pops, never smoothed.
2. Visible fingerprints and handmade texture, the craft IS the style.
3. Warm practical lighting; miniature depth of field.
4. Gags timed to the step: anticipation hold, pop, settle.

## Worked example

Banana-peel gag: 3 held anticipation frames, flail pop, settle, fingerprints visible throughout.

## API references

- `data/v1/prompt-templates.json`

## Pitfalls

- Smoothing the motion, you just deleted the entire style.
