# Direct Ghibli-style pastoral

## What this gets you

Your scenes will have that hand-painted, gentle, drifting-cloud calm, landscapes the audience wants to live inside.

## Use when

Contemplative, nature-driven work.

## Inputs

- Landscape plates
- A small human moment

## Procedure

1. Watercolor skies, painterly detail, soft light, baked into plates.
2. Held frames and gentle motion; let the world breathe.
3. Small character, big world: scale contrast is the emotion.
4. Sound: wind, grass, distant birds, the pastoral is heard.

## Worked example

Girl on a winding path, clouds drifting, 20s held wide, the audience exhales.

## API references

- `data/v1/prompt-templates.json`

## Pitfalls

- Rushing, Ghibli pacing is the style; fast cuts kill it.
