# Brief AI music (Suno-style)

## What this gets you

You will be able to ask an AI music tool for a score cue and actually get back something your film can use.

## Use when

Original score on an AI budget.

## Inputs

- Temp references
- Emotional map of the film

## Procedure

1. Brief per cue: genre, tempo, instrumentation, emotional job, what to avoid.
2. Generate 3–5 variants per cue; comp sections like a score.
3. Keep stems when possible — dialogues duck under full mixes badly.
4. Competition rule: AI-generated music with rights held — document it.

## Worked example

Climax cue brief: 'orchestral hybrid, 110 BPM, strings + pulse, dread → triumph, no choir.'

## API references

- `data/v1/competitions.json`

## Pitfalls

- One 3-minute generation as 'the score' — score is built cue by cue.
