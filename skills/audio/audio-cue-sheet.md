# Run the cue sheet

## What this gets you

You will have a working cue sheet (the Cue Sheet page) listing every scene's dialogue, music and sound layers, and a verdict on whether the mix is ready.

## Use when

At picture lock, before the mix, and at every mix session after, until the sheet reads clean and survives the silence test.

## Inputs

- Scene list in cut order
- Voice Bible per speaking character
- Dialogue route per scene (native or decoupled)
- Score cues with their emotional function

## Procedure

1. Work the five pre-mix gates in order: Voice Bible written for every speaking character; dialogue route chosen per scene and never mixed mid-scene; score briefed per cue to the locked cut; stems kept for score and voices; generation receipts saved for every AI voice and cue.
2. Log every scene with its three layers, the ambient bed, the foley, the accents, its dialogue route, and its state today: designed, thin, or dry.
3. Respect the mechanical rule: a scene with no bed reads dry whatever its owner claims, the bed runs under everything and never drops to digital silence. Build backgrounds first, hard FX second, foley third.
4. Work the build order in the mix: dialogue first, then the SFX bed, then music last. Music before SFX is the classic amateur mistake.
5. Close with the chapter's QC: the three-way listen (headphones, laptop speakers, phone, if the dialogue survives the phone, the mix works) and the silence test (mute the music; if the scene collapses, the music was doing the acting, fix the scene, not the score).

## Worked example

Worked example on the sheet (cuesheet.html): 'The Night Bus' across 5 scenes, 4 designed, the rain stop thin with its foley not yet laid, reads 'Hold the mix' until the foley is laid and the scene marked designed, then 'Mix clean'.

## Pitfalls

- Letting native model audio be the only sound layer, it's a bed, not a mix.
- Scoring to a moving target instead of the locked cut.
- Bouncing the score to a stereo pair before the mix is signed off, stems are the mix's breathing room.
