# Direct an AI voiceover

## What this gets you

Your AI narrator or character will sound directed — paced, emphasised, human — instead of reading like a satnav.

## Use when

Narration, documentary VO, essay films.

## Inputs

- Script
- Voice Bible per character/narrator

## Procedure

1. Cast the voice: register/timbre/tempo/manner in the Voice Bible.
2. Direct with tactics, not adjectives ('read it like a warning' beats 'read it ominously').
3. Generate per paragraph; comp the best takes like a real VO session.
4. Process: cleanup → EQ → compress → de-ess → level.

## Worked example

Essay-film narration: 14 paragraphs, 3 takes each, comped — indistinguishable from a directed session.

## Pitfalls

- One long take — paragraph-level generation gives you edit points.
