# Budget a 90-minute feature

## Use when

Feasibility planning for features.

## Inputs

- Rate, resolution, team size, licensed-talent needs

## Procedure

1. Clean pass: 5400s × $0.0738/s ≈ $400.
2. Realistic retail envelope: ~$2,000–$40,000.
3. Company productions ($500K–$1M compute) are internal infra + teams + talent — not retail.
4. Big swings: licensed likenesses, 4K, team salaries dwarf compute.

## Worked example

Disciplined indie feature at 720p: low tens of thousands retail compute; team and finishing dominate.

## API references

- `api/v1/models.json`

## Pitfalls

- Presenting internal infra costs as 'what a feature costs' — retail is 100× less.
