# Budget a 3-minute short

## What this gets you

You will know what a good 3-minute short really costs, in credits and dollars, before you generate the first shot.

## Use when

Planning a short before generating.

## Inputs

- Model mix + rates
- Shot count estimate (~40–60 shots)

## Procedure

1. Clean pass: 180s × rate (e.g., $0.35/s 720p = $63).
2. Apply 5–10× for competition grade: $315–$630 realistic.
3. Reserve 20% for the post-rough-cut supervision pass.
4. Draft at 480p/720p; heroes only at final res.

## Worked example

Seedance 720p: $63 clean → ~$500 realistic envelope with 8× overhead.

## API references

- `data/v1/models.json`

## Pitfalls

- Forgetting audio costs — small, but real at competition audio standards.
