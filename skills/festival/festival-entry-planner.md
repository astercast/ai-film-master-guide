# Plan a festival entry

## Use when

Deciding whether and how to enter.

## Inputs

- The festival record (prizes, criteria, timeline)
- Your concept

## Procedure

1. Score your concept against the four criteria: directing/storytelling, visual craft, sound/editing, originality.
2. Pick your lane: photoreal drama, authored style, or spectacle — and commit.
3. Budget backwards from the deadline: generation time + post + audit buffer.
4. Plan the submission package (watermark, packshot, social post) as deliverables, not afterthoughts.

## Worked example

Prizes: $500K/$200K/$100K + $100K audience + 10×$10K. Criteria-mapped plan for a 4-min authored-style drama.

## API references

- `api/v1/festivals.json`

## Pitfalls

- Entering without mapping to criteria — judges score the criteria, not your effort.
