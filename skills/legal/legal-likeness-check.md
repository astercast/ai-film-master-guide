# Verify likeness clearance

## Use when

Before generating any face or voice resembling a real person.

## Inputs

- Every face/voice reference in the film

## Procedure

1. Written commercial licensing agreement per performer: defined inputs (photos, audio), talent script approval, biometric data-deletion clause.
2. Voice is separately protected (e.g., Tennessee ELVIS Act covers voice simulations) — a face license is not a voice license.
3. Deceased persons: estate clearance required (family consent + compensation, per the Kilmer precedent).
4. Keep the license, the input-materials log, and the data-deletion record together.
5. No license = do not generate the person. Synthetic characters and Soul IDs are the safe default.

## Worked example

Cameo of a real streamer: licensed with 2 min of audio + photo set, 30-day data deletion — cleared.

## Pitfalls

- 'It's transformative' as a legal strategy — get the license or cut the likeness.
