# Run the disclosure pass

## Use when

Before publishing anywhere.

## Inputs

- The finished film
- The platform list

## Procedure

1. YouTube: 'Altered content' toggle for realistic AI-generated/altered content.
2. TikTok: AI-generated content switch for realistic-looking AI scenes or people.
3. Meta: AI-disclosure tool for photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio.
4. X: no switch — deceptive synthetic media may be labeled, demoted, or removed anyway.
5. Log it: what was disclosed, where, when — keep an AI disclosure log; inconsistent disclosure draws penalties.
6. Note: cloning your own voice, animation, and production assistance (script help, color) generally don't trigger disclosure.

## Worked example

Multi-platform short: toggles set on YT/TikTok/Meta, log filed — zero takedowns.

## Pitfalls

- Assuming one platform's toggle covers the others — each needs its own.
