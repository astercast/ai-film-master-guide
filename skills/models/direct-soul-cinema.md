# Direct with Soul Cinema

## What this gets you

You will know whether Soul Cinema is worth your money for the shot you have in mind — what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around — before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Soul Cinema (Higgsfield, image). Best use: Cinematic keyframes, close-ups, film-grain stills for I2V.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: In-app credit cost still unpublished — verify live. (API access is billed in USD, not credits: $0.0032 per image starting rate, from Higgsfield’s own API post))
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: In-app credit cost still unpublished — verify live. (API access is billed in USD, not credits: $0.0032 per image starting rate, from Higgsfield’s own API post). Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Prompt-driven cinematic look, natural grain, era-aware; feeds video models.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Preview status at time of research; image only.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
