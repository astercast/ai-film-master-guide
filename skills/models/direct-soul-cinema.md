# Direct with Soul Cinema

## Use when

You are generating on Soul Cinema (Higgsfield, image). Best use: Cinematic keyframes, close-ups, film-grain stills for I2V.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: not in captured table — verify live)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: not in captured table — verify live. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Prompt-driven cinematic look, natural grain, era-aware; feeds video models.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Preview status at time of research; image only.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at not in captured table — verify live: estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.
