# Direct with Soul 2.0

## What this gets you

You will know whether Soul 2.0 is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Soul 2.0 (Higgsfield, image). Best use: Fashion/portrait/aesthetic keyframes; character-sheet generation.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: 0.12 / image)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: 0.12 / image. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Style presets, Color Transfer, cultural fluency, Soul ID slot; 0.12 credits/image.
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: Image only; style range narrower than general models.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at 0.12 / image: estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.
