# Direct with Suno v5.5

## What this gets you

You will know whether Suno v5.5 is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Suno v5.5 (Suno, audio). Best use: Full songs for soundtrack moments and music-video work: structured tracks with vocals, stems, and a multitrack studio to arrange them.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Free tier (v4.5-all, no commercial use); Pro $10/mo; Premier $30/mo (adds Suno Studio); verify live.)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Free tier (v4.5-all, no commercial use); Pro $10/mo; Premier $30/mo (adds Suno Studio); verify live.. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Suno's current music model (v5, Sept 2025; v5.5, Mar 26, 2026, per Suno's own release notes): the most expressive vocal performances in the line, plus Voices (sing in your own verified voice), Custom Models trained on at least six of your own tracks (up to three per account), and My Taste personalisation. Suno Studio is a browser multitrack workstation with stem separation and MIDI export. Songs run up to about 8 minutes..
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: The rights posture is mid-transition: the Nov 2025 Warner Music settlement brings licensed models during 2026 and changed the language from owning outputs to holding commercial rights on paid tiers; read the current terms before a commercial release, and keep the guide's music-rights skill in the loop..
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
