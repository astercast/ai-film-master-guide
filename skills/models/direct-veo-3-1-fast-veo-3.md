# Direct with Veo 3.1 Fast / Veo 3

## Use when

You are generating on Veo 3.1 Fast / Veo 3 (Google DeepMind, video). Best use: Budget b-roll, fast iteration of Veo look.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Veo 3.1 Fast ~16/4s; Veo 3 720p ~58/8s; Veo 3 Fast ~22/8s)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Veo 3.1 Fast ~16/4s; Veo 3 720p ~58/8s; Veo 3 Fast ~22/8s. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Veo quality at ~40% credit cost.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Lower fidelity than full 3.1.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at Veo 3.1 Fast ~16/4s; Veo 3 720p ~58/8s; Veo 3 Fast ~22/8s: estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.
