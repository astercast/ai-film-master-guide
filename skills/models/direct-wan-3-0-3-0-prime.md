# Direct with Wan 3.0 / 3.0 Prime

## Use when

You are generating on Wan 3.0 / 3.0 Prime (Alibaba, video). Best use: Next-gen Wan quality when budget allows.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: 3.0: 480p 5, 720p 8.75, 1080p 17.5 /5s; Prime: 480p 7.5, 720p 15, 1080p 30 /5s)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: 3.0: 480p 5, 720p 8.75, 1080p 17.5 /5s; Prime: 480p 7.5, 720p 15, 1080p 30 /5s. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Newest Wan generation on Higgsfield.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Premium pricing; Prime 1080p = 30cr/5s.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
