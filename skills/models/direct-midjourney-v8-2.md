# Direct with Midjourney V8.2

## What this gets you

You will know whether Midjourney V8.2 is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Midjourney V8.2 (Midjourney, image). Best use: Cinematic keyframes and look development stills with the signature Midjourney grade, fed into image-to-video models.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Subscription only: plans from $10/mo (Basic) to $120/mo (Mega), billed in GPU time, verify live.)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Subscription only: plans from $10/mo (Basic) to $120/mo (Mega), billed in GPU time, verify live.. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Midjourney's current image model line: V8 alpha (Mar 17, 2026) rebuilt the model for speed (about 5x faster) and literal prompt adherence, with native 2K via HD mode; V8.1 became the default in June 2026; V8.2 became the default on July 24, 2026, focused on aesthetics and personalisation. The V8.2 Edit Model (public testing from Aug 27, 2026, per Midjourney's own updates blog) edits by instruction, inpaints and outpaints, and takes up to 4 image references, replacing omni-reference..
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: Subscription and GPU-hour budgeting instead of per-image API pricing; no official API; the edit model is still a public test, so behaviour shifts between visits..
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
