# Direct with Adobe Firefly

## What this gets you

You will know whether Adobe Firefly is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Adobe Firefly (Adobe, suite). Best use: Commercially safe generation inside the edit itself: Premiere Pro Generative Extend, Firefly Boards look development, and one subscription surface for the partner models this guide tracks.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Plan-bundled (Firefly Pro and Premium plans, with an unlimited-generation offer for subscribers in early 2026); per-generation rates vary by partner model, verify live.)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Plan-bundled (Firefly Pro and Premium plans, with an unlimited-generation offer for subscribers in early 2026); per-generation rates vary by partner model, verify live.. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Adobe's own family plus a hub: Firefly Image Model 5 and the Firefly Video model are trained on licensed content and sold as commercially safe, and the Video model powers Premiere Pro's Generative Extend (extend a shot at its head or tail, plus up to 10s of generated room tone). Firefly also hosts 30+ partner models; Adobe's April 2026 newsroom lineup names Nano Banana 2, Veo 3.1, Runway Gen-4.5 and Aleph, Luma Ray3.14, FLUX.2 [pro], Kling 3.0 and 3.0 Omni, ElevenLabs Multilingual v2, and Topaz Astra..
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: Adobe's own video model is a short-clip and extend tool, not a flagship long-take generator; partner-model availability and credit costs shift by plan and month, verify live..
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
