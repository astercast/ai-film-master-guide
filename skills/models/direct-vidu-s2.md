# Direct with Vidu S2

## Use when

You are generating on Vidu S2 (Vidu, video). Best use: Real-time interactive character video; live-stream editing.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Cheapest paid tier ~$0.05/clip reported — verify live)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Cheapest paid tier ~$0.05/clip reported — verify live. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: S2-Avatar: interactive character up to 720p; S2-Editing: changes a live stream as it plays.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Real-time-first design, not a cinematic hero-shot model; pricing thinly documented — verify live.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example
