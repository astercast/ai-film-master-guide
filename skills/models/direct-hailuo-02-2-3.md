## Use when

You are generating on Hailuo 02 / 2.3 (MiniMax, video). Best use: Emotional close-ups, subtle facial performance, clean stylized moments.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: 02: 768p ~6/6s (~$0.24/6s), 1080p ~10/6s; 2.3: 768p ~6/6s, 1080p ~10/6s, Fast 768p ~4/6s)

## Procedure

1. Budget first: 02: 768p ~6/6s (~$0.24/6s), 1080p ~10/6s; 2.3: 768p ~6/6s, 1080p ~10/6s, Fast 768p ~4/6s. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Expressive physical motion, prompt-to-action fidelity, official [camera command] syntax.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: 6s/10s only; silent variants; not for multi-shot.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
