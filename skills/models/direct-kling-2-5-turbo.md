# Direct with Kling 2.5 Turbo

## What this gets you

You will know whether Kling 2.5 Turbo is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Kling 2.5 Turbo (Kuaishou, video). Best use: Cheap drafts, fast start-frame animation.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: 720p ~4/5s (~$0.16/5s), 1080p ~6/5s)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: 720p ~4/5s (~$0.16/5s), 1080p ~6/5s. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Cheapest usable motion (~4cr/5s 720p).
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: No native audio; lower fidelity.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at 720p ~4/5s (~$0.16/5s), 1080p ~6/5s: estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.
