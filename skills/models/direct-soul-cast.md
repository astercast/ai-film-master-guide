# Direct with Soul Cast

## What this gets you

You will know whether Soul Cast is worth your money for the shot you have in mind — what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around — before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Soul Cast (Higgsfield, image). Best use: Consistent cinematic character identity / AI actors.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: ~0.125 / image (third-party))
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: ~0.125 / image (third-party). Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Archetype-based casting, 16:9, era control.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: One person per identity; ecosystem-locked.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at ~0.125 / image (third-party): estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.
