# Direct with Seedream 5.0 Pro

## What this gets you

You will know whether Seedream 5.0 Pro is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Seedream 5.0 Pro (ByteDance, image). Best use: Multi-image sets that must stay consistent: character turnarounds, product and wardrobe variations, posters and frames with real text in them.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Seedream 4.5 official API rate $0.04 per image; Seedream 5.0 Flash listed at $0.018 per image on OpenRouter (Oct 2026); 5.0 Pro per-image rate not captured, verify live.)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Seedream 4.5 official API rate $0.04 per image; Seedream 5.0 Flash listed at $0.018 per image on OpenRouter (Oct 2026); 5.0 Pro per-image rate not captured, verify live.. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: ByteDance Seed's image line: generation and instruction editing in one model, up to 14 reference images, 1K to 4K output. The 4.5 release (Dec 2025) made multi-image consistency the headline, holding subject, lighting, and colour across a set; 5.0 (Lite, Feb 2026) and 5.0 Pro (July 8, 2026, per BytePlus) add deep reasoning about the brief, precision regional edits, and multilingual text rendering..
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: Fast version churn (4.0, 4.5, 5.0, 5.0 Pro, 5.0 Flash inside a year), so pin the exact model ID per project; Pro pricing and limits vary by host, verify live..
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
