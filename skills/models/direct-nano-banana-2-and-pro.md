# Direct with Nano Banana 2 and Pro

## What this gets you

You will know whether Nano Banana 2 and Pro is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Nano Banana 2 and Pro (Google DeepMind, image). Best use: Keyframes, character sheets, and plates that need consistent characters across many stills; poster and title art with legible text.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Per-image API pricing is published in Google's Gemini API pricing table in tiers by resolution; this guide captured no rate it can print, verify live before budgeting.)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Per-image API pricing is published in Google's Gemini API pricing table in tiers by resolution; this guide captured no rate it can print, verify live before budgeting.. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: One family, two gears: Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, Feb 26, 2026) does Pro-level generation and editing at Flash speed, 512px to 4K, holding up to 5 characters and 14 objects consistent in one workflow; Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image, Nov 20, 2025) is the precision tier, with studio-quality control, accurate text rendering, and real-world knowledge grounded in Search. Both edit as well as generate, and 2 is the default image model across the Gemini app, Search, and Flow..
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: Two models share one nickname, so quotes and tutorials often mix their tiers; the Pro tier costs more and runs slower; SynthID and C2PA marks ride on outputs..
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
