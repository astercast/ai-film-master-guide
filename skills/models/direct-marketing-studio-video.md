# Direct with Marketing Studio video

## What this gets you

You will know whether Marketing Studio video is worth your money for the shot you have in mind — what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around — before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Marketing Studio video (Higgsfield, video). Best use: Talking-presenter ads with speech + lip-sync + product lock.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: ~60/12s, ~75/15s (third-party, verify live))
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: ~60/12s, ~75/15s (third-party, verify live). Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Only package that lip-syncs its own speech; URL-to-video.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Highest per-clip image/video cost; presenter ages skew 18-35.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at ~60/12s, ~75/15s (third-party, verify live): estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.
