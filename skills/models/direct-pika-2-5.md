## What this gets you

You will know whether Pika 2.5 is worth your money for the shot you have in mind — what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around — before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Pika 2.5 (Pika, video). Best use: Stylized/social content, animation styles, fast iteration.

## Procedure

1. Budget first: not in captured Higgsfield table — verify live; Pika native: 12-80 credits/gen by tier. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: 20+ animation styles, Pikaframes keyframes, Pikadditions/Pikaswaps, built-in lip sync; 25s 1080p.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Realism trails Veo/Kling; negative-prompt dependence.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
