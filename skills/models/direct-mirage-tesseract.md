# Direct with Mirage Tesseract

## Use when

You are generating on Mirage Tesseract (Mirage (formerly Captions), suite). Best use: Agent-driven editing and motion graphics; free local engine.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Local engine free (reported — verify live))
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Local engine free (reported — verify live). Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Agent-driven editing and motion-graphics suite with a free local engine.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Not a shot-generation model — it belongs to the edit/post stage, not the generation pool; young evidence base.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example
