# Direct with Grok Video / Video Edit

## Use when

You are generating on Grok Video / Video Edit (xAI, video). Best use: Fast social clips; video-to-video edits.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: ~7/5s; Edit ~10/5s)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: ~7/5s; Edit ~10/5s. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Speed; edit mode.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Less cinematic control documented.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

## Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at ~7/5s; Edit ~10/5s: estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.
