# Direct with Lyria 3 Pro

## What this gets you

You will know whether Lyria 3 Pro is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Lyria 3 Pro (Google DeepMind, audio). Best use: Structured score and song cues inside Google's ecosystem: a three-minute track with named sections, generated next to the video work in Gemini and Google Vids.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Bundled with paid Gemini plans (daily song allowances by tier) and in preview on Vertex AI; no per-track rate captured, verify live.)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Bundled with paid Gemini plans (daily song allowances by tier) and in preview on Vertex AI; no per-track rate captured, verify live.. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Google DeepMind's music model (Mar 25, 2026, announced on Google's own blog): up to 3-minute tracks at 48kHz with structural awareness, so a brief can name the intro, verse, chorus, and bridge and the model follows the map; vocals and instrumentals, lyrics in several languages, automatic cover art, and a SynthID watermark on every output. Available in the Gemini app, AI Studio, the Gemini API, Vertex AI (preview), Google Vids, and ProducerAI..
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: A shorter ceiling than Suno's song lengths and no stem-editing studio of its own; commercial-use terms for film work need a live check before release..
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
