# Direct with Seedance 2.5

## Use when

You are generating on Seedance 2.5 (ByteDance, video). Best use: Hero multi-shot sequences; Higgsfield default video model.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: API ~$2.31/10s 720p (reported Sept 2026 benchmark — verify live); Higgsfield promo ~80 videos on Plus/mo BytePlus API estimate ~$0.57/s at 1080p for 2.5 (third-party estimate — verify live).)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: API ~$2.31/10s 720p (reported Sept 2026 benchmark — verify live); Higgsfield promo ~80 videos on Plus/mo BytePlus API estimate ~$0.57/s at 1080p for 2.5 (third-party estimate — verify live).. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Omni reference mode + start-image; best continuity per community.
3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Premium credit cost; 30-day unlimited promos are business-plan only.
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
