# Direct with Seedance 2.5

## What this gets you

You will know whether Seedance 2.5 is worth your money for the shot you have in mind — what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around — before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Seedance 2.5 (ByteDance, video). Best use: Hero multi-shot sequences; Higgsfield default video model.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: API ~$2.31/10s 720p (reported Sept 2026 benchmark — verify live); Higgsfield promo ~80 videos on Plus/mo BytePlus API estimate ~$0.57/s at 1080p for 2.5 (third-party estimate — verify live). - Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero
