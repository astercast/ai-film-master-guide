# Direct with Topaz Astra

## What this gets you

You will know whether Topaz Astra is worth your money for the shot you have in mind, what it costs, what it is best at, and the weakness to design around, before you spend on generations.

## Use when

You are generating on Topaz Astra (Topaz Labs, suite). Best use: The final mile: upscale finished AI clips to 4K delivery, rescue a soft hero shot, and add interpolated slow motion without re-generating.

## Inputs

- Shot list with durations (cost basis: Astra Standard $19/mo promotional ($39 list) with 400 credits/mo (about 10 minutes of HD video); extra credits $0.10 each; also inside Topaz Studio at $34/mo; verify live (Topaz Labs pricing page, read Oct 2026).)
- Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
- Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

## Procedure

1. Budget first: Astra Standard $19/mo promotional ($39 list) with 400 credits/mo (about 10 minutes of HD video); extra credits $0.10 each; also inside Topaz Studio at $34/mo; verify live (Topaz Labs pricing page, read Oct 2026).. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
2. Lean into its strength: Topaz Labs' creative video upscaler, built specifically for AI-generated footage: Precise mode restores faithfully on its Starlight model, Creative mode imagines new detail at subtle or bold strength, Astra 2 (Apr 2026) added promptable upscaling with creativity and sharpness controls, and frame interpolation reaches 120fps and 8x slow motion. Output to 4K. Also reachable inside Adobe Firefly and Premiere..
3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: Creative mode invents detail, which can change faces and text, so hero shots get a frame-by-frame check after upscaling; cloud credits meter long runtimes quickly; Adobe announced an acquisition of Topaz Labs in June 2026 (reported), so plan names may move, verify live..
4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
