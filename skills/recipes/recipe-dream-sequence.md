# Shoot a dream sequence

## Use when

Dreams, memories, altered states.

## Inputs

- The emotion the dream externalizes
- 2–3 impossible images

## Procedure

1. Orbit + atmosphere carry the effect: slow 360s, haze, impossible light.
2. Logic of emotion, not physics — but keep ONE continuity thread (a color, an object).
3. Sound design: reversed elements, sub-bass, washed dialogue.
4. Enter and exit on match cuts — the seam is the art.

## Worked example

Grief dream: slow orbit around an empty chair, bioluminescent haze, her voice washed under.

- `data/v1/prompt-templates.json`

## Pitfalls

- Random surrealism — dreams need one logic, even if it's emotional.
