# Grade with scopes, not eyes

## What this gets you

Your colours will be right on every screen, not just yours, measured with the waveform and vectorscope tools the pros use.

## Use when

Every grading session.

## Inputs

- Scopes: waveform, vectorscope

## Procedure

1. Blacks: do they actually reach black? (AI loves milky shadows.)
2. Skin: does it sit on the skin-tone line?
3. Saturation: nothing clipping in any channel.
4. Trust the scopes, the built-in 'cinematic' grade is designed to flatter first glances.

## Worked example

Shot looked 'cinematic', waveform showed crushed blacks and clipped reds. Fixed in 5 minutes.

## Pitfalls

- Eyeballing on an uncalibrated laptop, the most expensive free monitor.
