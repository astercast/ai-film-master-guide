# Cut sound-first

## Use when

Before the fine cut.

## Inputs

- Dialogue/VO
- Music direction
- SFX needs

## Procedure

1. Build the radio edit: story must work with eyes closed.
2. Lay dialogue, then ambience bed, then SFX, then music.
3. Use J/L cuts at every transition; room tone under everything.
4. Lock the sound arc before finalizing picture.

## Worked example

A 4-min short's radio edit exposed a dead act 2 — fixed with two pickup shots instead of twenty.

## Pitfalls

- Picture-locking before the sound works — re-cutting picture to fix sound is backwards.
