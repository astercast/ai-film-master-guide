# Structure a trailer cut

## What this gets you

You will have a 60–90 second trailer shape — hook, escalation, title — that makes a short film feel like an event.

## Use when

Trailers, teasers, sprint productions.

## Inputs

- The film's three biggest moments
- A music track with dynamics

## Procedure

1. 0–10s: the hook — the single most arresting image + line.
2. 10–60s: escalation — raise the stakes every 10 seconds, never resolve.
3. 60–90s: title card on the button image. Silence or single hit before the title.
4. Cut on the music; let percussion do the pacing work.

## Worked example

SCRATCH's structure: cold-open impact → 40s of rising action on a riser → title smash on the drop.

## Pitfalls

- Explaining the plot — trailers promise, they don't summarize.
