Budget example: five finished clips Suppose you need five accepted five-second shots and allow two attempts for each. That is ten attempts, 50 generated seconds, and 600 base credits. Standard web plan: The workload fits the monthly allowance if you spend no credits elsewhere. The monthly invoice is $15; allocating it entirely to these five accepted clips gives $3 per accepted clip. Runway API: At $0.01 per credit, the base generation charge is $6, or $1.20 per accepted clip under the same assumption. This is a calculated budget, not a measured acceptance rate or a like-for-like subscription recommendation. The web app provides an authoring workflow; the API requires an integration. Include your actual editing and implementation needs before choosing on generation cost alone. If all ten attempts are rejected, neither plan has produced five deliverable clips. Record accepted footage separately from the credit balance. What can increase the charge? Repeated attempts. A wording change that triggers another generation adds another attempt. Approve the subject, framing, and motion brief before making a batch. Professional output formats. Runway documents a surcharge of five credits per second for ProRes or PNG sequence output. A five-second generation in one of those formats therefore uses 85 credits at the stated base rate: 5 × (12 + 5). Web access to these formats depends on the plan. HDR profiles have separate developer surcharges; check the exact format before generating. Avoid paying twice for a required export format Runway’s web export-format guide currently limits ProRes and PNG sequences to Max, legacy Unlimited and Enterprise. For Gen-4.5, choose the format in Tool Mode before generating. Workflows and Agent return MP4. An existing generation cannot be switched to another output format; Runway says to generate again. Budget example, not an output test: Ten five-second MP4 attempts use 600 base credits. Repeating all ten as ProRes adds 850 credits, bringing that scenario to 1,450. Choose the required master format before the first run. Regenerated outputs need another review; this arithmetic does not assume they match or pass your brief. Developer billing remains separate. The API professional-output guide uses an outputFormat parameter; ProRes and PNG add five credits per second, while HDR and high bit-depth formats have other surcharges. Do not transfer a web-plan entitlement or base MP4 quote to an API master deliverable. Other tools. Image creation, video editing, audio, and upscaling may have their own allowances or charges. The Gen-4.5 base rate is not a quote for a finished, captioned advertisement. Unused subscription capacity. A subscription can be economical when you use its included workflow and credits. If you only generate one clip in a month, dividing the price by the entire theoretical allowance understates what you spent for that clip. Does Gen-4.5 generate native 4K? The current Gen-4.5 help guide lists 720p generation. A plan&#x27;s 4K upscaling entitlement is a separate operation; it does not make every original Gen-4.5 generation native 4K. Max can make sense for sustained usage or required professional output features. Higher capacity does not prove better creative results for the same generation settings. Check rollover separately. Runway&#x27;s Pro-plan guide says monthly included credits reset while purchased additional credits do not expire. Its Max pricing card advertises one-month rollover. Frequently asked questions Can I use my Runway subscription credits in the API? Web subscriptions and developer billing are separate. Budget the balance for the product you will actually use. Is a five-second Gen-4.5 clip always $0.60? That is the published Runway API base charge at 12 credits/second and $0.01/credit. Extra output-format charges and other operations can change the total. A web subscriber pays a subscription invoice rather than this standalone price. Do 625 credits cover ten usable videos? They cover ten whole five-second base attempts, with 25 credits left, if nothing else uses the balance. The number of usable videos depends on the results and your requirements. Does the Free plan guarantee a Gen-4.5 trial? No. It has limited model access and a one-time balance. Check which models are available in the current free account before planning a trial around Gen-4.5. Can new users still buy Runway Unlimited? No. Runway&#x27;s Unlimited-to-Max notice says Unlimited stopped accepting new subscribers on June 1, 2026. Eligible legacy Unlimited subscriptions remain in place through November 30, 2026, then move to Max; Runway describes separate choices for monthly and annual subscribers. New buyers should compare Max&#x27;s 9,500 monthly credits and one-month rollover rather than planning around Explore Mode.
