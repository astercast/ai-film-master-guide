The cutting room.

A playable cutting room: type a weak prompt, roll the camera, and watch the scene get built shot by shot from the guide's real prompt library, then improve the prompt in the fix bay and watch the cut improve, take by take. Real templates, real model pricing, real cost math. The Cutting Room works on one scene; one prompt at a time belongs in the Workshop, and a whole film is planned in the Studio.
