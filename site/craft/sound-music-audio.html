Sound, Music & Audio

Sound is half the picture. Amateur AI films are silent; winners are designed. (Festival rules require AI-generated music, voice, and sound — this isn't optional.)

Native audio

Some video models generate audio natively with the clip. Rules:

  • Keep it when it's ambient texture: room tone, wind, rain, crowd murmur, footsteps.
  • Strip it when it's dialogue, music, or anything prominent — regenerate those deliberately.
  • Never let native audio be your only sound layer. It's a bed, not a mix.

Sound design (the layer that sells reality)

Every scene needs three layers:

  1. Bed: ambient tone of the location (room, street, forest, wind) — runs under everything.
  2. Foley: footsteps, cloth, object handling — the sounds that make movement feel physical.
  3. Accents: designed hits for story beats — the turn, the reveal, the button.

AI SFX tools can generate all three from text prompts. Describe sounds the way you describe shots: specifically and physically.

Score

  • AI music generators take prompts like: "ambient synth, 85 BPM, rising tension, no vocals."
  • Temp-track discipline: cut to a temp score first, then generate the final score to the locked cut — never score to a moving target.
  • No vocals unless the story needs them; score serves the story, never shows off.
  • The festival requires AI-generated music with rights you hold — keep the generation receipts.

Dialogue

  • When AI dialogue works: short lines, clean delivery, minimal background noise in the reference audio.
  • When it doesn't: long speeches, overlapping voices, emotional extremes. Write around these — visual storytelling over talk.
  • Lip-sync pipeline: generate the visual first, then sync: feed clean WAV audio into the platform's lip-sync (Pika and Runway both offer it; clean audio in = believable sync out).
  • Rule: if the line doesn't change the story, cut it. Festival shorts win on images.

The audio checklist

  • Ambient bed under every scene
  • Foley on all physical movement
  • Score generated to the locked cut
  • Dialogue minimal, clean, synced
  • Final mix: dialogue > score > bed (nothing fights the story)
  • All audio AI-generated with rights documented (festival requirement)
