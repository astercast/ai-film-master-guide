The Studio.

Plan my AI film — or my AI series. A standalone film (title, logline, genre and mood, target runtime, quality tier) or a serialized series (3–10 episodes in one of the five proven serialized formats) in — and the Studio builds the production packet: a shot list with per-shot prompts written from the guide's 47-template library, a model pick for every shot with the real credit math, a budget table that shows its work, and a shooting schedule. Series get a locked bible with prompt DNA that keeps every episode consistent, plus a self-contained HTML packet you can hand to a collaborator. Every number traces back to models.json or a template id.
