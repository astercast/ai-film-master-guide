The Studio.

Plan my AI film — or my AI series — or my microdrama run. A standalone film (title, logline, genre and mood, target runtime, quality tier) or a serialized series (3–10 episodes in one of the five proven serialized formats) in — and the Studio builds the production packet: a shot list with per-shot prompts written from the guide's 47-template library, a model pick for every shot with the real credit math, a budget table that shows its work, and a shooting schedule. A microdrama run in — and the Studio plans the run's economics from the documented $150-microdrama figures: clip budget (10–15s clip units), per-episode and run budgets against the $50/min benchmark, the 3–5× "drawing cards" retake budget, a character-regen reserve, and a timeline from the documented 4-days-for-4-episodes pace. Series get a locked bible with prompt DNA that keeps every episode consistent, plus a self-contained HTML packet you can hand to a collaborator. Every number traces back to models.json or a documented figure — ranges, not promises.
