Model deep dive

Runway Act-Two

Runway · video

Active last verified 2026-10-15 ANSWER PRINT

Best use
Acted scenes with generated characters: direct the performance yourself on camera, then put it on any character. The fastest route to expressive, repeatable acting in AI film, and the standard fix for the dead faces pure text-to-video still produces.
Cost
About $0.05/s at Runway's API credit price ($0.01 per credit): Act-Two bills 5 credits per second of output, 3s minimum, on Runway plans and Runway Dev. MuAPI lists performance transfer at $0.07 per generation. Verify live.
Strength
Performance capture without a stage: shoot a driving performance on any camera, give Act-Two a character image or video, and it transfers the performance onto the character, facial expressions, head rotation, hand gestures and full-body movement. With image inputs a gesture-control setting decides how much hand and body motion carries over, and environmental motion is synthesised so the character sits inside the scene instead of floating on it. Up to 30s at 24 fps, the clip lengths working scenes actually need.
Weakness
One performer per pass, and the driving video needs a clearly visible face with no cuts; the performance you get is the performance you shot, it cannot invent acting. Output resolution sits below the current generation flagships, and it is a transfer tool, not a generator: no scene exists until a generator or a camera makes one.

What changed: NEW, added 2026-10-15 (completeness coordinator addendum): a standing Runway first-party model (Runway Dev's proprietary line is Gen-4.5, Aleph 2.0 and Act-Two) that no lane of the completeness wave carried, despite performance capture being a core AI-film technique with its own skill family in this guide. Specs and rate verified against October 2026 guide coverage on 2026-10-15.

Prompt tips for this model

  • One action per shot. Video models execute a single believable verb well; stacked actions collapse, directing performance and the failure doctor show what breaks.
  • Run it through the linter. Paste any prompt for this model and check all ten layers before spending credits.
