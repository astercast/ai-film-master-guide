Wan 3.0 / 3.0 Prime

Alibaba · video

Active last verified 2026-10-11 ANSWER PRINT

Best use
Next-gen Wan quality when budget allows
Cost
3.0: 480p 5, 720p 8.75, 1080p 17.5 /5s; Prime: 480p 7.5, 720p 15, 1080p 30 /5s ; provider-list API: $0.05/s at 480p, 2 to 30s with native audio (reported benchmark, verify live) Prime Singapore list (therundown, Aug 2026, verify live): $0.068/s 480p, $0.14/s 720p, $0.28/s 1080p. Third-party gateways: APIMart $0.0329 480p / $0.0658 720p / $0.1315 1080p; Atlas Cloud $0.04/s.
Strength
Newest Wan generation on Higgsfield
Weakness
Premium pricing; Prime 1080p = 30cr/5s

What changed: Launched Aug 24, 2026 (closed weights). Third-party resync notes (Aug 2026) report a ~38% price cut on base 3.0 and confirm 3.0 Prime (up to 5x faster iteration), verify live pricing. Oct 2026 third-party field notes rate Wan 3.0 the budget/fast-drafting pick (from ~6 credits/shot on aggregator hosts). Oct 2026 fuel refresh: formal release confirmed Aug 24, 2026; 2 to 30s with native audio at $0.05/s at 480p (provider list price), 'thirty seconds with audio got cheap; a year ago that length and price did not exist together'. Re-verified 2026-10-11: formally released Aug 24, 2026; remains Alibaba's current flagship. Launch spec: 30s @ 30fps, 480p/720p/1080p, native audio by default, up to 10 image + 5 video + 5 audio references, document-to-video mode (WinBuzzer via versely). Hosted rows (e.g. Versely) cap at 15s, the 30s number is Alibaba's spec, not every host's. Re-verified 2026-10-11: formally released Aug 24, 2026 (public beta since Aug 6). Native 30-second single generation at 30fps, not stitched clips; 480p/720p/1080p; up to 10 image, 5 video, and 5 audio references; a document mode turns PDFs, decks, or webpages into video. Alibaba Cloud Model Studio: $0.05/s 480p, $0.10/s 720p, $0.20/s 1080p (Zacks, Aug 2026).

Cost math

  • 1080p ≈ 17.5 credits / 5s, one finished minute ≈ 210 credits (≈ $8.23); a 3-minute short ≈ $24.70 before takes.
  • 1080p ≈ 30 credits / 5s, one finished minute ≈ 360 credits (≈ $14.11); a 3-minute short ≈ $42.34 before takes.

USD at $0.0392/credit (Higgsfield Plus plan, observed 2026-10-10). Verify live before budgeting, rates change.

Prompt tips for this model

  • One action per shot. Video models execute a single believable verb well; stacked actions collapse, directing performance and the failure doctor show what breaks.
  • Run it through the linter. Paste any prompt for this model and check all ten layers before spending credits.
  • Not sure it's the right model? Take the 5-question quiz or compare it side-by-side with alternatives.
