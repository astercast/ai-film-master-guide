Kling 3.0

Kuaishou · video

Active last verified 2026-10-11 ANSWER PRINT

Best use
Motion-heavy cinematic clips, action, multi-shot, camera direction
Cost
720p ~7/5s (~$0.55/8s), 1080p ~8/5s, 4K ~30/5s; Motion Control 720p ~7, 1080p ~12 ; API $0.84/10s 720p (reported Sept 2026 benchmark) Kling API docs pricing: kling-3.0-turbo $0.112/s 720p, $0.14/s 1080p (audio included); kling-v3 $0.084/s std, $0.112/s pro, $0.126-$0.168/s with audio, $0.42/s 4K; kling-v2-5-turbo $0.042/s 720p, $0.07/s 1080p (1 unit = $0.14) — third-party mirror of official docs, rechecked Oct 2026.
Strength
Best-in-class motion/physics; native audio; Motion Control puppetry; Omni multi-shot; 15s
Weakness
Subtle emotional faces trail Hailuo; fingers/camera drift without control; probabilistic

What changed: Kling 4.0 announced Sept 28, 2026 (Flash early access for Ultra Yearly subscribers; full release expected Oct 2026, not yet GA as of Oct 11). 3.0 remains the current GA model. 3.0 Omni (kling-o3) confirmed as a distinct first-frame variant. API benchmark $0.84/10s 720p (reported, Sept 2026). Included in Adobe's Sept 8 in-Premiere multi-model workbench. Re-verified 2026-10-11: still the current GA model. CORRECTION, updated to the board's r82 state (research cycle 81, AI Video r82 — the board's first move since r63): on the Artificial Analysis text-to-video board the Kling 3.0 family now sits at #15 (Kling 3.0 Omni, 1015) and #16 (Kling 3.0 Pro, 1000); the September claim that Kling 3.0 ranked 12th (not 4th) is superseded — the board itself was re-polled for r82, and the full r82 table is quoted in the field chapter. kling-3.0-turbo GA June 17, 2026 (audio always on).

Cost math

  • 720p ≈ 7 credits / 5s — one finished minute ≈ 84 credits (≈ $3.29); a 3-minute short ≈ $9.88 before takes.
  • 1080p ≈ 8 credits / 5s — one finished minute ≈ 96 credits (≈ $3.76); a 3-minute short ≈ $11.29 before takes.
  • 4K ≈ 30 credits / 5s — one finished minute ≈ 360 credits (≈ $14.11); a 3-minute short ≈ $42.34 before takes.

USD at $0.0392/credit (Higgsfield Plus plan, observed 2026-10-10). Verify live before budgeting — rates change.

Prompt tips for this model

  • Direct the voice, not just the picture. Native-audio models live or die on voice direction — sound, music & audio covers TTS direction, Voice Bibles, and the two dialogue roads.
  • One action per shot. Video models execute a single believable verb well; stacked actions collapse — directing performance and the failure doctor show what breaks.
  • Run it through the linter. Paste any prompt for this model and check all ten layers before spending credits.
  • Not sure it's the right model? Take the 5-question quiz or compare it side-by-side with alternatives.
