Wan 2.7

Alibaba · video

Active last verified 2026-10-12 ANSWER PRINT

Best use
Directed mid-budget clips where the prompt is the storyboard: timed multi-shot prompts, reference-held characters and products, lip-synced talking heads from a supplied audio track, and prompt-guided edits of footage you already have — the videoedit endpoint is the one no neighbouring model in this price band matches.
Cost
Alibaba Cloud Model Studio list (USD): $0.10/s at 720p, $0.15/s at 1080p — the same rate on every wan2.7 video endpoint; fal hosts the same card at $0.10/s (720p) and $0.15/s (1080p). China list: ¥0.6/s at 720p, ¥1.0/s at 1080p. Worked: a 5s clip at 720p runs $0.50; 10s at 1080p runs $1.50; the full 15s at 1080p runs $2.25. Billed per generated second — verify live.
Strength
Thinking Mode: the model plans the composition before it generates, which shows up as fewer artifacts and steadier multi-shot prompts — a prompt can carry timed shot changes (Shot 1 / Shot 2 / Shot 3 with time ranges) and the model cuts on them. Full four-endpoint suite under official IDs (wan2.7-t2v, wan2.7-i2v, wan2.7-r2v, wan2.7-videoedit): text-to-video, image-to-video with first/last-frame lock and continuation, reference-to-video with up to 5 reference images, and prompt-guided editing of existing footage. 2-15s at 720p/1080p, native audio in the same pass, precise color control (HEX codes and palettes) and strong character consistency across references.
Weakness
1080p ceiling — no 4K; the top rung of the Wan line is 3.0. Closed weights: no local run (the open-weights Wan story still ends at 2.2). Native audio is always on — some hosts offer no silent option. Reference-to-video and videoedit cap shorter than text-to-video on some hosts (2-10s against 2-15s), so the row you rent is not always the spec sheet.

What changed: NEW — added to model watch 2026-10-12 (Watchtower 2.4) through the research-artifact chain, closing the gap flagged in wave 21 and verified in Watchtower 2.3. Launched April 2026 (Tongyi Lab) with Thinking Mode and the four-endpoint suite at 2-15s, 720p/1080p, native audio. It sits between Wan 2.6 (the camera-first 2.x line) and Wan 3.0 (the 30-second flagship): the last 2.x generation, and the first Wan with a published USD list card the budget tools can read directly.

Prompt tips for this model

  • Direct the voice, not just the picture. Native-audio models live or die on voice direction — sound, music & audio covers TTS direction, Voice Bibles, and the two dialogue roads.
  • One action per shot. Video models execute a single believable verb well; stacked actions collapse — directing performance and the failure doctor show what breaks.
  • Run it through the linter. Paste any prompt for this model and check all ten layers before spending credits.
  • Not sure it's the right model? Take the 5-question quiz or compare it side-by-side with alternatives.
