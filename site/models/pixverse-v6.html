AIsphere · video

Best use

Cost

Strength
Native audio generated in the same pass; 1-15s clips at up to 1080p; 20+ prompt-driven camera controls (focal length, aperture, depth of field, lens distortion); multi-shot flag cuts one prompt into an ordered scene; reference-to-video with up to 10 images; strong independent image-to-video showings (Artificial Analysis Elo 1,343, April 2026).

Prompt tips for this model

  • Direct the voice, not just the picture. Native-audio models live or die on voice direction — sound, music & audio covers TTS direction, Voice Bibles, and the two dialogue roads.
  • One action per shot. Video models execute a single believable verb well; stacked actions collapse — directing performance and the failure doctor show what breaks.
  • Run it through the linter. Paste any prompt for this model and check all ten layers before spending credits.
  • Not sure it's the right model? Take the 5-question quiz or compare it side-by-side with alternatives.
