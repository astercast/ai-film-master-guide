Black Forest Labs · video

Active last verified 2026-10-11 ANSWER PRINT

Best use
Dialogue-heavy scenes, multi-shot single takes, typography-in-video; draft-then-finish cost control
Cost
API: $0.06/s 720p draft, $0.17/s 720p, $0.29/s 1080p (~$2.90/10s HD), verify live

Up to 20s single take with native 1080p and native multilingual audio + lip-sync; start/end frames and ordered keyframes; multi-shot generation and video continuation in one take; strong typography; draft mode for cheap iteration; Arena text-to-video #2 (1496 pts, behind Gemini Omni Flash 1512)

Headline preference stats are vendor-run, not independent; native 2K/4K upscale and open weights promised as 'coming soon', verify live

Prompt tips for this model

  • Direct the voice, not just the picture. Native-audio models live or die on voice direction, sound, music & audio covers TTS direction, Voice Bibles, and the two dialogue roads.
  • One action per shot. Video models execute a single believable verb well; stacked actions collapse, directing performance and the failure doctor show what breaks.
  • Run it through the linter. Paste any prompt for this model and check all ten layers before spending credits.
  • Not sure it's the right model? Take the 5-question quiz or compare it side-by-side with alternatives.
