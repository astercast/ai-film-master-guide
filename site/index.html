00:01:00:00Scene 01two ways to shoot

Pick your track

Two ways to shoot. The craft overlaps; the prompt language doesn't. Pick the lane your film lives in.

Animated / Stylized

Anime, Ghibli-style, Pixar-style 3D, 2D cartoon, claymation. Cheaper to iterate, more forgiving on faces, judged in its own categories — with its own prompt register (no lens or depth-of-field language) and its own model shortlist.

Prompt engineering Model selection

Photorealistic

Premium models, identity discipline, and post-production craft that passes for real. The lane for drama, action, and trailer-grade work — where every shortcut shows.

Soul ID deep dive Character consistency

00:08:00:00Scene 05a note, taped to the bin

From the cutting room

M1 · good take

If you read one chapter this week, make it prompt engineering. Everything downstream — the budget, the edit, the grade — gets cheaper when the prompt is right on the first take. And skip the model-comparison rabbit hole until you've finished three clips on whatever you already have. The model is never the reason the film is bad; the cut is.

— the editor
