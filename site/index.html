First reading order: by track

Animated / Stylized

Two ways to shoot. The craft overlaps; the prompt language doesn't. Anime, Ghibli-style, Pixar-style 3D, 2D cartoon, claymation. Cheaper to iterate, more forgiving on faces, judged in its own categories, with its own prompt register (no lens or depth-of-field language) and its own model shortlist.

Prompt engineering · Model selection

Photorealistic

Premium models, identity discipline, and post-production craft that passes for real. The lane for drama, action, and trailer-grade work, where every shortcut shows.

Soul ID deep dive · Character consistency

From the cutting room

If you read one chapter this week

Make it prompt engineering. Everything downstream, the budget, the edit, the grade, gets cheaper when the prompt is right on the first take. And skip the model-comparison rabbit hole until you've finished three clips on whatever you already have. The model is never the reason the film is bad; the cut is.
