How the room works 00:03:45:00

The room reads structure, not taste. Six layers — someone to watch, something happening, a camera decision, light, place and time, a feeling — scored 0–100 from the words on the page. Then the money rule: festival ambition only unlocks flagship pricing when the read clears 55. A cold read gets draft money no matter what was asked for, and the room says so on the record.

Every shot is built from the guide's 47-template prompt library and priced from the real per-second pricing table — low/high ranges, the tier priced, the arithmetic shown per shot. Envelopes are 5× and 20× the clean number. Post (assembly, audio, grade) is human time and software you already own; it isn't modeled. Ranges, not promises — verify live before spending.

The room also does hype hygiene: Veo 4 doesn't exist (the “release date” pages are SEO bait — verified Oct 2026), Kling 4.0 is announced but not shipped, and Sora is discontinued. Name one and the room corrects the record instead of pricing the fantasy.
