Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 5

Pick the serialized format for a series

Gets you: You will pick the episode shape that fits your idea, mini-drama, point-of-view series, day-in-the-life, revenge arc, or audience-decides, with its length and story skeleton set.

What this is
Choose among the five proven serialized formats (mini-drama, POV series, day-in-the-life, revenge arc, interactive) or custom, each with its runtime band and beat skeleton.
First step
Match the concept to a format: mini-drama (60-150s, one emotion, 3-5 beats, turn, cliffhanger); POV series (30-90s, 'you' are the protagonist); day-in-the-life (15-60s, recurring AI character vignette); revenge arc (60-150s, betrayal -> justice ladder); interactive (60-150s, one decision per episode, comment/poll beat, cliffhanger at the next choice).
You will need
Series concept + target platform (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (4 steps; estimated from the procedure itself, your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner, made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 189 of 257: Lock a mini-drama series bible), but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof: the worked example
Revenge arc for a design-theft story: 60-150s band, 15s/beat x 4 beats = 60s in-band; emotion triumph; cliffhanger 'he spots her in the crowd, freeze'.

Use when

When the series concept exists but the episode shape doesn't. Format is a completion-rate decision, not a vibe.

Inputs

  • Series concept + target platform (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)

Procedure

  1. Match the concept to a format: mini-drama (60-150s, one emotion, 3-5 beats, turn, cliffhanger); POV series (30-90s, 'you' are the protagonist); day-in-the-life (15-60s, recurring AI character vignette); revenge arc (60-150s, betrayal -> justice ladder); interactive (60-150s, one decision per episode, comment/poll beat, cliffhanger at the next choice).
  2. Apply the format's beat skeleton to the first episode block: emotion, stake, hook, beats, turn, cliffhanger, keyframe scenes, seconds-per-beat.
  3. Custom is a real choice, it just has no preset skeleton; define the band yourself and hold it.
  4. Verify: beats x seconds-per-beat lands inside the format's band; one emotion per episode (the two-emotions rule).

Worked example

Revenge arc for a design-theft story: 60-150s band, 15s/beat x 4 beats = 60s in-band; emotion triumph; cliffhanger 'he spots her in the crowd, freeze'.

API references

  • data/v1/checklists.json

Pitfalls

  • Two emotions per episode, completion rate dies when the episode can't decide what it is.
  • Beats x seconds-per-beat outside the band, the runtime estimator flags it; fix the beat count, not the band.
