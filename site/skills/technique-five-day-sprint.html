Proof: the worked example
SCRATCH: director/musician + 3D/VFX generalist, five days, mistaken for a theatrical trailer.

Reference skill, kept for the record, for agents, and for later: not a first-project skill. See SKILLS_AUDIT.md (rotation 20).

Use when

You have days, not weeks, and deep tool fluency.

Inputs

  • Trailer structure (hook/escalation/title, 60 to 90s)
  • Music track first
  • 3 to 5 variations budgeted per hero shot

Procedure

  1. Cut a trailer, not a short, music-driven pacing, three acts.
  2. Keep shots 2 to 4 seconds; short shots hide consistency breaks.
  3. Build sound in parallel, percussion, risers, hard SFX sell 'studio' more than pixels.
  4. Decide selects fast. Fluency beats deliberation at sprint speed.

Worked example

SCRATCH: director/musician + 3D/VFX generalist, five days, mistaken for a theatrical trailer.

API references

  • data/v1/models.json

Pitfalls

  • Sprinting without fluency, five days of hesitant prompting produces nothing shippable.
