Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 4

Cut sound-first

Gets you: You will have a version of your film that already works with your eyes closed, dialogue and sound cut first, picture fitted to it.

What this is
Assemble the radio edit before locking picture.
First step
Build the radio edit: story must work with eyes closed.
You will need
Dialogue/VO; Music direction; SFX needs
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (4 steps; estimated from the procedure itself, your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner, made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 168 of 257: Decide what to upscale), but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof: the worked example
A 4-min short's radio edit exposed a dead act 2, fixed with two pickup shots instead of twenty.

Use when

Before the fine cut.

Inputs

  • Dialogue/VO
  • Music direction
  • SFX needs

Procedure

  1. Build the radio edit: story must work with eyes closed.
  2. Lay dialogue, then ambience bed, then SFX, then music.
  3. Use J/L cuts at every transition; room tone under everything.
  4. Lock the sound arc before finalizing picture.

Worked example

A 4-min short's radio edit exposed a dead act 2, fixed with two pickup shots instead of twenty.

Pitfalls

  • Picture-locking before the sound works, re-cutting picture to fix sound is backwards.
