Prefer video references over text

Gets you: Your scenes will continue from what is actually on screen — showing the model the previous video instead of describing it and hoping.

What this is
Show, don't tell — the chaining rule.
First step
Attach finished video as the reference, not a text description.
You will need
Previous scene's finished video
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (3 steps; estimated from the procedure itself — your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner — made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 132 of 241: Use face-erased sheets for new angles) — but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof — the worked example
Scene 8 generated against scene 7's final — wardrobe, light, and room all hold.

Prefer video references over text

Use when

Scene-to-scene continuity.

Inputs

  • Previous scene's finished video

Procedure

  1. Attach finished video as the reference, not a text description.
  2. Text drifts over generations; video doesn't.
  3. Still re-anchor original plates every few scenes.

Worked example

Scene 8 generated against scene 7's final — wardrobe, light, and room all hold.

Pitfalls

  • Describing the last scene in words — elegant, wrong.
