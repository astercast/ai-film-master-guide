Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 6

Assemble the paperwork stack

Gets you: When a distributor or festival asks for your papers, you will hand over the complete stack in one go — ownership proof, insurance, AI records — and the deal will not stall.

What this is
Gather the legal papers a distributor or festival will ask for — proof you own the film, insurance, and the AI-specific records (likeness licences, generation logs) no standard form covers.
First step
Chain of title: script rights, contributor agreements, location releases, music licenses + cue sheet, clearances, distribution agreements.
You will need
The finished film; All production records
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (5 steps; estimated from the procedure itself — your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner — made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 229 of 241: Clear AI music rights properly) — but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof — the worked example
Short acquired by a distributor: chain of title + E&O + AI folder delivered — no closing delays.

Use when

Before any distributor conversation or festival submission — assembling this late is the expensive version.

Inputs

  • The finished film
  • All production records

Procedure

  1. Chain of title: script rights, contributor agreements, location releases, music licenses + cue sheet, clearances, distribution agreements.
  2. E&O insurance bound and maintained ~3 years post-release — confirm with the broker that coverage maps to AI-generated material (it may not qualify for copyright protection).
  3. AI-specific folder: likeness licenses, platform subscription records + generation logs, music license file + cue sheet, AI disclosure log.
  4. No union performer releases needed for synthetic 'performers' — they're not performers; the likeness license is what matters.
  5. Bring an entertainment attorney in early — retrofitting paperwork after the fact is the expensive version.

Worked example

Short acquired by a distributor: chain of title + E&O + AI folder delivered — no closing delays.

Pitfalls

  • Missing chain-of-title documents — they can block broadcast, streaming, or sale outright.
