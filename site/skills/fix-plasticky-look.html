Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 6

Fix the plasticky AI look

Gets you: Footage that screams 'AI', waxy skin, candy colours, will come out looking filmed instead.

What this is
Kill over-smooth, over-saturated generative rendering.
First step
Desaturate: pull saturation 10 to 20% (Lumetri or equivalent).
You will need
The offending clip
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (4 steps; estimated from the procedure itself, your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner, made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 211 of 241: Fix bad lip sync), but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof: the worked example
Before/after: -15 saturation, 35mm grain overlay, 2px CA, reads as 16mm film.

In plain words

the AI look
AI look, The over-saturated, over-smooth, plasticky signature of raw generative output; killed in post with grain, aberration, and desaturation.

Use when

Any footage that reads as 'AI' instead of photographed.

Inputs

  • The offending clip

Procedure

  1. Desaturate: pull saturation 10 to 20% (Lumetri or equivalent).
  2. Add fine film grain, the fastest anti-plastic pass.
  3. Add subtle chromatic aberration at high-contrast edges.
  4. Check skin: if waxy, a skin-refinement pass or slight diffusion helps.

Worked example

Before/after: -15 saturation, 35mm grain overlay, 2px CA, reads as 16mm film.

Pitfalls

  • More sharpening, plastic + sharp = worse. Soften, don't sharpen.
