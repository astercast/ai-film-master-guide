Use when

You are generating on ElevenLabs Eleven v3 (ElevenLabs, audio). Best use: Directed voice performance: narration, character dialogue rendered line by line, and dubbing, plus music and sound effects from the same platform.

Inputs

  • Shot list with durations (cost basis: Credit subscriptions: Starter $5/mo, Creator $22/mo (unlocks professional voice cloning), Pro $99/mo, with higher tiers above; failed generations still consume credits, verify live.)
  • Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
  • Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

Procedure

  1. Budget first: Credit subscriptions: Starter $5/mo, Creator $22/mo (unlocks professional voice cloning), Pro $99/mo, with higher tiers above; failed generations still consume credits, verify live.. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
  2. Lean into its strength: Eleven v3 (left alpha in Feb 2026; general availability across ElevenLabs platforms on Mar 14, 2026, per the company's announcement): Audio Tags give inline performance direction in brackets, 70+ languages hold one voice identity, Text to Dialogue renders multi-character scenes in one pass with matched prosody, and complex-text errors fell 68% against the prior tier. Flash v2.5 remains the low-latency tier. The same platform adds Eleven Music and a sound-effects generator, so voice, score, and spot effects share one account..
  3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: v3 is a render-quality model, not a real-time one; ElevenLabs itself routes conversational work to Flash v2.5. Voice cloning carries consent and disclosure duties; see the guide's legal chapter before cloning anyone..
  4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
  5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
