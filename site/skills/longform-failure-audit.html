Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 5

Audit long-form failure modes

Gets you: You will catch the nine ways long AI films usually fall apart while yours can still be fixed cheaply.

What this is
Check a production against the nine documented long-form failure modes.
First step
Drift accumulation: compare character faces shot 1 vs shot 50.
You will need
Current cut or selects
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (9 steps; estimated from the procedure itself — your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner — made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 192 of 241: Run the five post stages) — but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof — the worked example
Audit found style decay in act 2 — fix: re-baked location plates, re-generated 6 shots, locked.

Use when

At picture lock, and any time quality slips mid-production.

Inputs

  • Current cut or selects

Procedure

  1. Drift accumulation: compare character faces shot 1 vs shot 50.
  2. Style decay: optics/grade wandering — is the look baked into plates?
  3. Blocked-position flips: screen sides mirroring?
  4. Memory-less prompting: any 'same as before' in prompts?
  5. Summoning by negation: banned objects appearing anyway?
  6. Reference beats prompt: daylight refs overruling evening prompts?
  7. Budget: weak scenes generated before stress-testing?
  8. Ordinary-scene trap: is the dialogue coverage actually harder than the spectacle?
  9. Subtle-acting ceiling: are performances directed as eye-work and tactics, not emotion labels?

Worked example

Audit found style decay in act 2 — fix: re-baked location plates, re-generated 6 shots, locked.

Pitfalls

  • Auditing only the spectacle — the ordinary rooms are where long-form dies.
