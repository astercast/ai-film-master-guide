Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 5

Shoot an establishing sequence

Gets you: You will be able to tell an audience where they are, when it is, and how to feel — in under 15 seconds.

What this is
Place, time, tone in under 15 seconds.
First step
Aerial or wide pull-back revealing geography (dawn mist optional but effective).
You will need
Location; Time of day; The tone word
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (4 steps; estimated from the procedure itself — your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner — made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 172 of 241: Cut a montage) — but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof — the worked example
Coastal town, dawn: drone pull-back + fishing boat detail, 12 seconds, cool grade.

Use when

Openings and act transitions.

Inputs

  • Location
  • Time of day
  • The tone word

Procedure

  1. Aerial or wide pull-back revealing geography (dawn mist optional but effective).
  2. One detail shot that says 'people live here.'
  3. Time-of-day baked into the plate; tone in the grade.
  4. Under 15 seconds — establishing is a door, not a room.

Worked example

Coastal town, dawn: drone pull-back + fishing boat detail, 12 seconds, cool grade.

API references

  • data/v1/prompt-templates.json

Pitfalls

  • The 45-second travelogue opening — judges fast-forward it.
