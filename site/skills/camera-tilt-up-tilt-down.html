Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 1

Direct: Tilt up / Tilt down

Gets you: Your shot will gain what this move is for, revealing height or depth; horror/suspense reveals; establishing scale, in the exact words models obey, ready to paste into any prompt.

What this is
Direct a tilt up / tilt down camera move with exact prompt phrasing.
First step
Start from the guide's phrasing: "Camera tilts cautiously, revealing [doorway]" / Hailuo: [Tilt up] / [Tilt down]
You will need
Subject label or @ reference the move is motivated by; Speed and stability (slow/majestic, energetic/handheld, locked); The story purpose of the move in one sentence
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (5 steps; estimated from the procedure itself, your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner, made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 23 of 241: Direct: Pan left / Pan right), but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof: the worked example
"Camera tilts cautiously, revealing [doorway]" / Hailuo: [Tilt up] / [Tilt down]

Use when

Use when the shot needs what this move does: Revealing height or depth; horror/suspense reveals; establishing scale.

Inputs

  • Subject label or @ reference the move is motivated by
  • Speed and stability (slow/majestic, energetic/handheld, locked)
  • The story purpose of the move in one sentence

Procedure

  1. Start from the guide's phrasing: "Camera tilts cautiously, revealing [doorway]" / Hailuo: [Tilt up] / [Tilt down]
  2. State the move, the speed, AND the purpose, models obey motivated camera better than decorative camera.
  3. Keep it the only move in the prompt. One move per shot.
  4. Pair with a matching subject pace (tracking a runner = camera pace matches runner).
  5. Draft at 720p; check the move reads before committing to hero resolution.

Worked example

"Camera tilts cautiously, revealing [doorway]" / Hailuo: [Tilt up] / [Tilt down]

API references

  • data/v1/camera-movements.json

Pitfalls

  • Naming the move without speed/stability, 'dolly in' alone gives you a random dolly.
  • Combining with a second move, pick one.
