Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 3

Choose a model for music videos

Gets you: You will know which model cuts to a beat without falling apart — style, speed and rhythm in one pick.

What this is
Beat-driven visuals with style velocity.
First step
Cut on the beat — visuals change with the music, not against it.
You will need
Track BPM and dynamics; Performance vs abstract ratio
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (3 steps; estimated from the procedure itself — your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner — made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 117 of 241: Choose a model for stylized 3D) — but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof — the worked example
3-min track at 120 BPM: ~60 shots, 3s each, style-locked, beat-mapped in the edit.

Use when

Music videos, visualizers, performance pieces.

Inputs

  • Track BPM and dynamics
  • Performance vs abstract ratio

Procedure

  1. Cut on the beat — visuals change with the music, not against it.
  2. Style velocity over fidelity: bold looks, fast cuts, 2–3s shots.
  3. Budget for volume: music videos eat generations; draft cheap.

Worked example

3-min track at 120 BPM: ~60 shots, 3s each, style-locked, beat-mapped in the edit.

API references

  • data/v1/models.json

Pitfalls

  • One look for three minutes — music videos die of visual monotony.
