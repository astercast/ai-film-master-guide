Direct with Midjourney V1 Video

Use when

You are generating on Midjourney V1 Video (Midjourney, video). Best use: Stylized/animation shots from stills; distinctive Midjourney aesthetic in motion.

Inputs

  • Shot list with durations (cost basis: ~8x image cost; 5s SD batch = 8 GPU-min; verify live)
  • Whether you need native audio, lip sync, or long takes
  • Your quality bar: draft (720p/fast) vs hero

Procedure

  1. Budget first: ~8x image cost; 5s SD batch = 8 GPU-min; verify live. Multiply by 3–5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
  2. Lean into its strength: Signature Midjourney look in motion; simple Animate workflow; extend to 21s.
  3. Design around its weakness — do not fight it: Image-to-video only; 480p/720p; no native audio; plan sound separately.
  4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
  5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.

Worked example

10-second hero shot budgeting at ~8x image cost; 5s SD batch = 8 GPU-min; verify live: estimate the per-second rate, × 2 for duration, × 4 for re-roll headroom.

API references

  • data/v1/models.json

Pitfalls

  • Weakness to respect: Image-to-video only; 480p/720p; no native audio; plan sound separately.
  • Costs in models.json were refreshed 2026-10-11 — verify live before spending real money.
