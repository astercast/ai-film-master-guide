Prompt: Tactics-driven dialogue beat

Gets you: You will have a finished prompt for a tactics-driven dialogue beat, the shot used for dialogue coverage, ready to paste into a video model: fill the bracketed blanks, generate a cheap draft first, and keep the take that works.

What this is
Write a tactics-driven dialogue beat prompt (performance track) using the guide's template.
First step
Gather one value per placeholder. Keep [STYLE] identical to every other prompt in the project.
You will need
[ENV], environment description; [LIGHT], lighting direction; [LINE], fill per the template's anatomy; [SHOT], shot size; [STYLE], your style-bible line, verbatim; [SUBJECT], locked identity label or @ reference
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (7 steps; estimated from the procedure itself, your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner, made to be usable on a first project.

Use when

You are directing a shot that needs: tactics-driven dialogue beat. Anatomy: tactic + line + one physical action = playable direction. Best for: dialogue coverage.

Inputs

  • [ENV], environment description
  • [LIGHT], lighting direction
  • [LINE], fill per the template's anatomy
  • [SHOT], shot size
  • [STYLE], your style-bible line, verbatim
  • [SUBJECT], locked identity label or @ reference

Procedure

  1. Gather one value per placeholder. Keep [STYLE] identical to every other prompt in the project.
  2. Fill the template verbatim, do not paraphrase its camera language:
  3. [SHOT] of [SUBJECT] at [ENV], [LIGHT]. [SUBJECT] says "[LINE]" with tactic [TACTIC: to accuse], physical action [ACTION: leaning in, controlled]. Subtext lives in the body, not the face, no mugging. [STYLE]. [AUDIO: dial...
  4. Confirm exactly ONE primary motion and ONE camera move in the final prompt.
  5. Separate identity from motion: appearance descriptors in the Identity Block, action in the Motion Block, never pronouns alone as the subject anchor.
  6. Add native-audio direction (quoted, attributed dialogue; named SFX) when the model supports it.
  7. Generate a 720p draft first; approve the select before any 1080p/4K render.

Worked example

Filled draft: medium close-up of MARA at rain-slick alley, neon reflections. MARA says "[LINE]" with tactic [TACTIC: to accuse], physical action [ACTION: leaning in, controlled]. Subtext lives in the body, not the face, no mugging. 1970s anamorphic 35mm, warm tungsten, halation, fine grain. [AUDIO: dialogue + room tone.]

API references

  • data/v1/prompt-templates.json
  • data/v1/camera-movements.json

Pitfalls

  • Stacking two motions or two camera moves in one prompt, the model will split the difference and ruin both.
  • Changing the [STYLE] line between shots, the fastest route to a recast-looking cast.
