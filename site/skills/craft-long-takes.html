Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 4

Prefer long takes over cut-stitching

Gets you: Your scenes will flow in long unbroken shots instead of stitched fragments — solved while generating, not faked in the edit.

What this is
Flow solved at generation beats flow faked in post.
First step
Design the take as one continuous action + one camera path.
You will need
The sustained action
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (3 steps; estimated from the procedure itself — your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner — made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 136 of 241: Lay the ambience bed) — but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof — the worked example
20s market oner: tested at 10s, committed at 20s — no stitching needed.

Use when

Sustained sequences.

Inputs

  • The sustained action

Procedure

  1. Design the take as one continuous action + one camera path.
  2. Test at half length before committing full.
  3. Cut-stitching is the backup, not the plan.

Worked example

20s market oner: tested at 10s, committed at 20s — no stitching needed.

Pitfalls

  • Stitching five clips and calling it a oner — the seams always show.
