Guide / Skills / The Path, stage 1

Prompt: Car commercial

Gets you: You will have a finished prompt for a car commercial, ready to paste into a video model: fill the bracketed blanks, generate a cheap draft first, and keep the take that works.

What this is
Write a car commercial prompt (core track) using the guide's template.
First step
Gather one value per placeholder. Keep [STYLE] identical to every other prompt in the project.
You will need
[CAR], fill per the template's anatomy; [ROAD], fill per the template's anatomy; [STYLE], your style-bible line, verbatim
Time
About 3 minutes for a first run-through (7 steps; estimated from the procedure itself, your model’s generation time is extra).
Level
Beginner, made to be usable on a first project.
Before this
Best after the skill before it on the Path (skill 13 of 257: Prompt: Underwater reveal), but everything it needs is listed below, and nothing else is assumed.
Proof: the worked example
Filled draft: Low-angle tracking shot of [CAR] on [ROAD] at blue hour, [LIGHT: headlight bloom, city glow]. Smooth gimbal. 1970s anamorphic 35mm, warm tungsten, halation, fine grain.

Use when

You are directing a shot that needs: car commercial. Anatomy: low angle = power; smooth move = luxury. Models: Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1.

Inputs

  • [CAR], fill per the template's anatomy
  • [ROAD], fill per the template's anatomy
  • [STYLE], your style-bible line, verbatim

Procedure

  1. Gather one value per placeholder. Keep [STYLE] identical to every other prompt in the project.
  2. Fill the template verbatim, do not paraphrase its camera language:
  3. Low-angle tracking shot of [CAR] on [ROAD] at blue hour, [LIGHT: headlight bloom, city glow]. Smooth gimbal. [STYLE].
  4. Confirm exactly ONE primary motion and ONE camera move in the final prompt.
  5. Separate identity from motion: appearance descriptors in the Identity Block, action in the Motion Block, never pronouns alone as the subject anchor.
  6. Add native-audio direction (quoted, attributed dialogue; named SFX) when the model supports it.
  7. Generate a 720p draft first; approve the select before any 1080p/4K render.

Worked example

Filled draft: Low-angle tracking shot of [CAR] on [ROAD] at blue hour, [LIGHT: headlight bloom, city glow]. Smooth gimbal. 1970s anamorphic 35mm, warm tungsten, halation, fine grain.

API references

  • data/v1/prompt-templates.json
  • data/v1/camera-movements.json

Pitfalls

  • Stacking two motions or two camera moves in one prompt, the model will split the difference and ruin both.
  • Changing the [STYLE] line between shots, the fastest route to a recast-looking cast.
