Use when

You are generating on Grok Imagine Video 1.5 (xAI, video). Best use: Cheap high-volume social video; native-audio dialogue clips.

Procedure

  1. Budget first: API ~$0.08/sec output (~$4.80/min); verify live Lite (Oct 2026, verify live): $0.02/s 480p to $0.14/s 1080p; $0.01/image input; 1-15s clips; 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 3:2 +1. xAI API docs (rechecked Oct 2026): Lite $0.02/s at 480p, $0.03/s at 720p, $0.14/s at 1080p, +$0.01 per input image; flagship 1.5: $0.08/s at 480p, $0.14/s at 720p, $0.25/s at 1080p (third-party mirror of official docs, verify live).. Multiply by 3 to 5× per hero shot for re-rolls.
  2. Lean into its strength: Native audio (dialogue/SFX/music) in one pass; 6-15s clips extendable to 30s; topped the image-to-video arena at launch (third-party).
  3. Design around its weakness, do not fight it: 720p (1080p added July 2026); voice-reference gating; younger evidence base.
  4. Draft everything at the cheapest tier that still answers the creative question.
  5. Upscale or re-render at hero quality only for approved selects.
