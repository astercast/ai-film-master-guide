Guide / Economics

Credit Math

Professional quality without burning the budget. The standing rule: never spend credits on a guess.

What things cost (Higgsfield)

PlanCredits/moRough video yield
Basic120~15 standard clips
Pro600~75 standard clips
Max1,800~225 standard clips

Model tiers: Free (Soul 2.0, DoP Lite) · Low (0.1–2 credits) · Mid (2–10) · Premium (10+). A standard Kling clip runs ~8 credits; premium/longer/Cinema Studio sessions cost more.

Going direct to Kling: ~$0.084/sec at 720p standard, ~$0.112/sec pro, ~$0.42/sec native 4K. Credits reset each billing cycle and don't roll over — schedule production so paid credits don't evaporate.

The no-guess pipeline (credit-saving order of operations)

  1. Write the shot list first. Every clip planned before a single credit is spent.
  2. Draft cheap. Turbo/Lite models for composition and motion tests.
  3. Render heroes expensive. Premium models only for faces, dialogue, and key story beats.
  4. One clip at a time, quality-gated. A failed clip never chains — re-prompt, don't hope.
  5. Price before you send. Estimate cost per request before batching; verify balance before a batch, not after.

Where to spend vs save

  • Spend: hero close-ups, dialogue shots, the trailer moment, the final shot.
  • Save: establishers, B-roll, transitions, montages — Turbo is fine.
  • Free tools that don't look free: CapCut for color/LUTs/grain, stabilization in post instead of re-rendering, compositing fixes (face swaps from close shots) instead of regenerating wide shots.

The festival budget frame

A 3–5 minute festival film at ~4 seconds per shot is 45–75 shots. At draft-then-hero discipline, most shots need 2–4 generations to land. Budget for 150–300 generations and plan the credit pool before starting — or split across platforms by shot type.

Rules of thumb from working filmmakers

  • ~$80 per finished minute of film, all-in.
  • 3–5 takes per keeper; 2–5x raw-to-finished multiplier. Budget the rejects, not the hero renders.
  • $40–165 in credits for a disciplined 3-minute short; $150–300+ without discipline.
  • Resolution ladder: 480p drafts → 720/1080p heroes → upscale to 4K in post (native 4K ≈ $1/sec).
  • Audio-first: lock voiceover/soundtrack before generating video — cheap audio prevents expensive timing-mismatch re-rolls.
  • Cautionary: one documented Higgsfield pilot burned ~$260,000 in credits over 4 days for ~8 usable shots — brute-force iteration without craft. The no-guess rule exists because of math like this.
