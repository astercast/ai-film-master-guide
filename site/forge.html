The cutting room

Episode forge.

One episode on the anvil: an eight-beat, three-act arc from the guide's craft chapters, with a real library template hammered onto every beat it fits — and an honest gap named where none does. Export the plan and shoot it.

Set the episode

One episode, one emotion, eight beats. The Forge lays the arc the craft chapters teach, then walks the guide's own template library beat by beat and attaches the template that fits — scored on the template's own facets (shot type, camera movement, mood) and its stated use. A beat the library does not cover is named, not papered over.

Episode 1 · Mini-drama · one emotion: longing · 8 of 8 beats covered by the library.

Act 1 — Cold open

1

The hook

t1 · match 3

Hook line in the first 3 seconds; caption and opening shot say the same thing (genre anatomy, Episodic Short-Form).

Establishing drone shot — openings, transitions

starts wide → reveals geography

Aerial pull-back over [ENV], dawn mist lifting, [LIGHT: golden hour backlight]. Slow, majestic. [STYLE]. [AUDIO: wind, distant birds.]

Shortlisted models: Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.0

Field lesson — Dreams of Violets: Ground invented footage in documented references. For a docudrama, the reference still IS the research — collect it like a researcher, lock it like a cinematographer. Teardowns

2

The stake

t2 · match 8

One want, visible on screen: the protagonist wants something concrete (Story Structure — the short-film engine).

Dialogue two-shot (shot-reverse-shot pair) — conversations

coverage, not chaos

Medium two-shot of [SUBJECT A] and [SUBJECT B] at [ENV], [LIGHT]. Static locked-off. [SUBJECT A] says, "[LINE]." [STYLE].
Close-up of [SUBJECT B] reacting, shallow depth of field, [LIGHT]. Static. [SUBJECT B] replies, "[LINE]." [STYLE].

Shortlisted models: Veo 3.1 (ingredients), Seedance 2.0 multi-shot, Kling 3.0

Field lesson — ONEIRIC: Block dialogue like a stage play before you generate. A simple color-coded map beats a paragraph of spatial description in every prompt. Teardowns

Act 2 — Escalation

3

Escalation

t5 · match 6

The want meets the obstacle; each shot raises the stakes or deepens the world (Story Structure — the judged-short template).

Action tracking shot — chases, action

camera pace matches subject pace

Tracking shot alongside [SUBJECT] [ACTION: sprinting through rain-slick alley], [LIGHT: neon reflections, volumetric haze]. Handheld energy, motion blur. [STYLE].

Shortlisted models: Kling 3.0, Luma

Field lesson — Passport Rush: Storyboard everything, even if you can't draw. Boards are the cheapest generation you'll ever make — a bad board costs minutes, a bad generation costs credits. Teardowns

4

The turn

t3 · match 8

One turn — the moment everything changes; a dedicated shot for the emotional turn (Story Structure; gate G4, Episodic Short-Form).

Emotional close-up — emotional peaks

ration close-ups (micro-acting is the weakest link)

Extreme close-up of [SUBJECT], eyes glistening, [LIGHT: soft window light, cool shadows]. Slow push-in. Micro-expression: restrained grief. [STYLE]. [AUDIO: room tone, faint breath.]

Shortlisted models: Hailuo 02/2.3, Veo 3.1

5

The complication

f5 · match 7

The plan fails or the truth lands — pressure builds before the reversal (Story Structure — the judged-short template).

Listening reaction — the other half of dialogue — shot-reverse-shot coverage

listening shots sell dialogue; AI defaults to blank — direct the arc

Close-up of [SUBJECT] listening, [LIGHT: soft key]. Micro-expression arc: neutral → [EMOTION: dawning concern] across the line. Small head tilt, breath catch, eyes drop then return. [STYLE]. [AUDIO: faint room tone, partner's muffled line.]

Shortlisted models: Hailuo 02/2.3, Veo 3.1

Act 3 — Reversal & button

6

The reversal

f2 · match 5

The reversal: the choice gets made. Most AI-film losers are tech demos with no second act — this is the second act (Story Structure).

Dead-eyes fix — give the eyes a job — reaction shots, listening coverage

eyes need a job every 2–3 seconds or they die on screen

Close-up of [SUBJECT] [ACTION: listening], [LIGHT]. Eyes move with purpose: glance to [FOCUS POINT], blink on the thought change, micro-reaction at [BEAT]. Never hold a glassy stare. [STYLE].

Shortlisted models: Hailuo 02/2.3, Veo 3.1

Field lesson — ONEIRIC: Block dialogue like a stage play before you generate. A simple color-coded map beats a paragraph of spatial description in every prompt. Teardowns

7

The button

a6 · match 5

One final image that reframes everything before it. End on the image, not on explanation (Story Structure — the judged-short template).

Oil-painting painterly motion — 

Oil painting come alive: [SCENE] rendered in thick impasto brushstrokes, paint swirls as clouds move, canvas texture visible. STYLE LOCK: painterly, visible brushwork, impasto, no photorealism.
8

The cliffhanger

r1 · match 7

Ends mid-beat or on an unanswered question — never the resolution. The resolution is what Episode N+1 is (genre anatomy, Episodic Short-Form).

Face-morph rescue — fixing face morphs

shorter + locked frame + static camera starves the morph

RESCUE for a morphing face: shorten to [DURATION: 3s], lock the start frame [REFERENCE], static camera, [SUBJECT] holds [SIMPLE ACTION]. Exclude: morphing faces, shifting features, extra limbs. If it still morphs, regenerate from the locked frame — do not extend.

Shortlisted models: any image-to-video (Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1)
